NovaBank Loan Document Generator

NovaBank's loan-document service — a REST microservice that fills Word (DOCX) templates from a JSON payload to produce the Nova Auto Loan and Nova Flex Loan bundles (54 fields total). It is a pure templater: it inserts caller-supplied values verbatim and performs no financial computation, so legal/finance review stays in control. Fill the form to see the request and a rendered preview, then download the document.

Why no auto-computation?

Interest, effective rate, documentary stamp tax, and deductions are not calculated by the service — they arrive pre-computed from the upstream system after legal/finance review. The service's only job is faithful placeholder replacement, which keeps the output auditable and the compliance boundary clean.
