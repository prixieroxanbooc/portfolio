Try the Google MCP servers with your email

Your email is your key. In the real system the gateway maps your email to your authorized Google credentials and the server acts as you. Enter any Google email below to generate a personalized sample. No real Google data is accessed — demo only.

How the gateway routes a call

  1. Assistant calls a tool with a profile key.
  2. Gateway maps key → identity (user email).
  3. Injects x-api-key: identity to the upstream server.
  4. Server loads that user's OAuth token and acts as them.
  5. Denylist + safe-defaults block destructive tools unless explicitly enabled.
