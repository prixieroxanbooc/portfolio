Stack & approach

Patterns I reach for repeatedly across these projects.

Serverless first

Stateless containers on managed serverless runtimes — scale to zero, pay per request, no servers to patch.

Data stays server-side

Heavy data work (hundreds of thousands of rows) happens in the service; clients and AI contexts only ever see results, never raw records.

Secure by default

Identity-aware proxy at the edge, role-based access inside, secrets in a managed vault, and an audit trail on sensitive actions.

Deterministic over magic

For anything financial or compliance-sensitive, logic is explicit and repeatable — no AI guessing at numbers at runtime.
